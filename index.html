Handicap upsets

First home is not first

Handicaps reshuffled both fleets. In the cats the quickest boats on the water had the hardest time on corrected, and the slowest rated boat on the course won.

Class against class

Which boats did the day suit?

Best boat from each class, measured against its fleet winner. In the cats the pattern is clean: the slower the rating, the better the result. In the dinghies the mid rated boats did best, with the fastest rated (29er, Fireball) and the slowest (Gypsy, RS Feva) furthest back.
